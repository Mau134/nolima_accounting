Nolima Accounting 1.1.0

- Quotations: create, print and turn a quotation into an invoice in one click (Sales > Quotations).
- Discounts: a discount % on any line of an invoice, quotation, cash sale or bill.
- Tourism levy (1%): added automatically to sales and posted to Tourism Levy Payable. Rate in Settings > Company.
- New report: Tourism levy summary (levy charged, paid and still owed, by month and department).
- Withholding tax: deduct WHT when paying suppliers (owed to MRA, payable annually) or record WHT withheld by
  customers. New report: Withholding tax summary.
- Part payments: take a part payment while creating an invoice; receipts show the balance still due.
- Bank and mobile money details printed on invoices, quotations, receipts and statements
  (enter them in Settings > Company).
- Item codes are now automatic (STK-0001 for stock, SRV-0001 for services).
- Faster: the dashboard, lists and searching are much quicker, especially with a lot of data.
