Nolima Accounting 1.3.0

- New invoice and quotation layout, like the lodge's own quotation: logo, Date / Total / Expiration boxes,
  dates on each line, one TAX line and a TAX SUMMARY showing Ministry of Tourism and MRA together.
  Upload your logo in Settings > Company.
- Numbering can continue from your existing numbers (e.g. Quote 1878): Settings > Company.
- Delete customers, suppliers, items and quotations that have not been used. Records with history
  can be hidden instead (tick "Show hidden" to see them).
- Profit is now calculated from expenses: stock bought counts as an expense straight away.
  Quantities in stock are still tracked. (Settings > Company > How profit is calculated.)
- VAT default for new companies is 17.5%.
