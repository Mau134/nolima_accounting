Nolima Accounting 1.2.0

- VAT and tourism levy are now calculated on the subtotal and added on top, so they are exactly 16.5% and 1%
  of the subtotal on every invoice, quotation and cash sale. Prices are always entered before VAT and levy.
- Bank details: up to three banks plus mobile money, printed on all customer documents
  (new tab: Settings > Bank details).
- Withholding tax is typed in by hand on customer invoices: when creating the invoice, or later with
  Sales > Withholding tax. Invoices show "Less: withholding tax" and the balance the customer still owes.
  The rate choices were removed from payments.
- Also in 1.1: quotations, line discounts, 1% tourism levy with its summary report, part payments,
  automatic item codes and a faster dashboard and lists.
